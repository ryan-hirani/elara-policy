Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-17

Private tool. Not for public use. Elara is a personal application operated by its owner for the owner alone. It is not available to, and may not be used by, anyone else.

Who uses Elara

Elara is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner and operator, Ryan Hirani. No other user can sign in. No data from anyone other than the owner is collected.

What Google data Elara accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, Elara reads and writes the owner's Google Tasks. Later versions may, again only with the owner's consent, read the owner's Google Calendar events, Gmail messages, Contacts and Drive files to prepare summaries and create tasks.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to provide the assistant's features to the owner: creating, listing and completing tasks, summarising the day, and answering the owner's own questions about his own information. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or training of machine-learning models.

Where the data lives

Data is processed on a server controlled by the owner. Access tokens are stored encrypted at rest on that server and are never shared. Short excerpts of the owner's data may be sent to a language-model provider (Anthropic) under its no-training API terms in order to produce a reply, and are not retained there beyond the provider's stated abuse-monitoring window.

Sharing

Elara does not sell, rent, or share data with third parties. There are no analytics or advertising trackers on this site or in the app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Elara's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Task data lives in the owner's Google account and follows Google's retention. Elara keeps logs for up to 90 days. The owner can revoke Elara's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates its tokens; stored tokens are then deleted from the server.
