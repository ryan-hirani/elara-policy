Elara

Elara is a private, single-user assistant built and operated by Ryan Hirani for his own use. It helps its owner capture tasks, read his own calendar and mail, and prepare a daily brief.

Elara is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and serves exactly one account: its owner's. This page exists so that the Google services Elara connects to can show where the app comes from and how it handles data.
